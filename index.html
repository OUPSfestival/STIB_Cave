Artistic Research Conflicts

The Ssymnac Cave Complex

by Manuel Phil. Bischof

This fictional archive explores the relationship between documentation, memory, and historical interpretation. By introducing invented materials into an archival framework, the project investigates how narratives of the past are produced and legitimized.

Public Dialogue invites visitors to share interpretations, questions, and alternative readings of the archive. Contributions become part of the ongoing discussion surrounding each object.
