// DESCRIPTION

This site is the digital archive and discussion platform for the diorama presented in the group exhibition “Espèces Compagnes” at Espace Qartier, Brussels 2026.

by Manuel Phil. Bischof

Through stories, artefacts, and dialogues, they explore symbiotic relations between minerals, beings, technologies, and humans. The project examines how narratives reshape historical “facts,” construct “reality,” and influence possible futures.roject explores the power of narratives: how historical “facts” change through time and how they shape “reality” and our future.
